# Applywright — design and copy direction

Truth is `docs/SPEC.md`: score one CV against one advert, explain every point, rewrite only from facts already there, prepare the pack, the person submits. The hero in `public/index.html` is a dark gradient (`hero__grad`), “Your CV, tailored for every job.”, and a fake browser around a 78 ring plus a grounded before/after. The subcopy is already the better sentence.

28 Oct 2026.

## 1. Current, and already costume

A competent page is now cheap to generate, so looking current is no longer a distinction ([ANODA](https://www.anoda.mobi/ux-blog/top-ui-design-trends-for-2024-standout-user-interface-practices)). Settled baseline:

- **Type does the hierarchy.** One family, a real display size, hairlines instead of boxes. Loud type only if it survives large text. [Bubble (30 Jul 2026)](https://bubble.io/blog/web-design-trends/) calls the quiet version barely-there UI and ties it to trust products.
- **Motion explains one change, then stops.** Reduced motion gets the finished state. Scroll-jacking is fading (Bubble).
- **The picture is the document**, including empty, error and a long CV (ANODA).
- **Panel size means priority.** Equal rounded cards are the old grid with a nickname. Bento is established (Bubble; [The Plus Addons, 21 Sep 2026](https://theplusaddons.com/blog/web-design-trends-2026/)).
- **Contrast, focus, real headings.** On a marketing page, glass is a sticky bar, never body copy (The Plus Addons).

Costume, including this hero. Gradient blobs on near-black (ANODA). Glass stacks, lime-on-charcoal, sparkles, “AI-powered”. Logo walls: a numbered quote beat logos by 35% in one reported test ([Quanta Intelligence, citing UserEvidence/Mutiny, 17 Oct 2026](https://quantaintelligence.ai/blog/how-to-write-landing-page-copy-that-converts)). Three-dot chrome and a match-rate ring; Jobscan owns the dial. Brutalism, Y2K and kinetic type on every line belong on studio reels ([Setproduct, 5 Jun 2026](https://www.setproduct.com/blog/retro-brutalist-ui-design-2026)). Inter at one radius is the template AI builders already shipped to everyone.

## 2. Three moves

The page should be the work.

1. **Type like a marked CV.** Old-style figures on the document, tabular figures on the score, mono only on the grounding line. The CV should look more serious than the marketing.
2. **Advert beside CV.** A short advert extract with missing terms marked; the same bullet before and after, surviving numbers untouched. No fake browser. Panel size follows the finding.
3. **One motion.** Each deducted point opens as a named finding, then stops. Reduced motion shows the list already open. If it does not mean “this is why it is 78”, cut it.

## 3. Ten slogans

Seven words or fewer. Scores are 1–5 for clarity, distinctiveness, and whether a sceptical applicant would believe it. Teal, Rezi, Enhancv, Kickresume and Apply4Me already tailor to an advert ([Apply4Me, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.apply4me.io/en/best-ai-resume-builders)).

| Slogan | Words | Clear | Distinct | Believed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Tailored from your CV. Nothing invented. | 6 | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| Your numbers stay. The wording moves. | 6 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Every score point has a reason. | 6 | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| Match the advert. Keep your facts. | 6 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| The pack is ready. You still apply. | 7 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| See why this CV misses the job. | 7 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Rewrite the CV. Don’t invent the career. | 7 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Show the gaps. Fix only real ones. | 7 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Prepared for this advert. Sent by you. | 7 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| A score you can argue with. | 6 | 3 | 5 | 3 |

“Pack” is jargon until the letter and screening answers are on screen. “Argue with” is deniable. “Misses the job” speaks only to people already rejected.

## 4. The one to use

**Your numbers stay. The wording moves.**

[Spike (1 Jul 2026)](https://getspike.ai/blog/saas-landing-page-best-practices/): if three rivals can use the sentence unchanged, it is a category label. “Your CV, tailored for every job.” fails. Teal, Rezi and Enhancv can run it tomorrow.

The replacement is a check the free plan can prove, and the rule in `SPEC.md`: numbers, employers, titles and dates stay; wording, order and emphasis may move. The hero’s 18% bullet is the proof. “Nothing invented” is a virtue claim rivals will copy and then break. Put it under the grounding check, as evidence.

Keep the eyebrow and the subcopy. It already says the person sends the application.

## 5. Three copy rules

1. **Paste-test every heading.** If Jobscan, Teal or Enhancv could ship it, name a finding, a kept fact, or the click the human still makes. Outcome in their words, mechanism second ([Orbix, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.orbix.studio/blogs/saas-website-best-practices); [Rob Palmer, 9 Mar 2026](https://robpalmer.com/blog/saas-landing-page-copy)).
2. **One worked example beats a logo wall.** One advert, one bullet, the numbers that survived, the finding that cost the points. No invented interview rates, and no “dream job”.
3. **Say the refusal once, beside the button.** No auto-send, no invented job, no scraped LinkedIn. Cut career multipliers and any claim the free plan cannot show in one session.
