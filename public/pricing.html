The small print, in plain English

Plan changes happen on the billing screen inside the app, once you are signed in. Creating an account starts on the free plan and charges nothing.

VAT

What you pay

Every price on this page is quoted before tax. The plan price is the amount billed; VAT, where it applies, is not included in these numbers.

Cancel

Cancel at any time

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not delete your account, your CVs or the packs you have already built.

Downgrade

Downgrade keeps packs readable

If you move down a plan, every pack you already built stays readable and exportable. Quotas fall to the new plan, so a full CV library may need trimming before you add another CV.

Billing questions

Does the free plan need a card?

No. Creating an account starts the free plan and charges nothing, so no payment details are needed to score a CV, build three packs a month or save one alert.

What counts against the monthly pack quota?

One tailored pack, built once, whatever it contains: the print-ready CV, the covering letter and the screening answers. Reopening a pack you already built does not use another one. Scoring a CV without building a pack does not either.

Why is the annual price ten months for twelve?

Paying for a year up front is simpler for us to bill, so the discount is passed on: £190 against twelve monthly payments of £19, and £490 against twelve monthly payments of £49. Both annual prices are exactly ten monthly payments.
