## 1. ATS reality, and an audit of our own claims

Modern stacks separate **parsing**, **recruiter string filtering** and **criteria-based AI review**.
Textkernel segments a CV into typed JSON and invokes OCR only when configured
([data model](https://developer.textkernel.com/tx-platform/v10/resume-parser/data-model/),
[OCR](https://developer.textkernel.com/tx-platform/v10/resume-parser/ocr/)); iCIMS maps parsed sections
into profiles ([docs](https://developer-community.icims.com/platform/docs/resume-parsing)). Recruiters
mostly filter on **exact literal strings** — Greenhouse's talent filtering does not match
"customer support" for a search on "customer service"
([Greenhouse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/35568627456667-Talent-Filtering-overview)).
Newer assistive review is criteria-based with written explanations, not an opaque score
([Ashby](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/34882962157083-AI-Assisted-Application-Review)).

**Folklore to avoid:** "75% of CVs are auto-rejected by bots" traces to a defunct 2012 vendor release.
Greenhouse `Auto-Reject` can only fire on structured knockout questions
([docs](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360054771791-Application-auto-reject)); Enhancv's
2025 study of 250 hiring managers found ~8% reported automated rejection without human review and 6–11s
first skims ([study](https://enhancv.com/blog/how-recruiters-read-your-resume-study/)). Automated
rejection also engages UK GDPR Art. 22 and EU AI Act Annex III(4). Text-based PDFs parse fine; only
scanned PDFs need OCR. And at scale, generic AI covering letters correlate with **51% fewer callbacks**
([arXiv:2509.25054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25054)) — which supports our refusal to invent.

### Claims on our pages that the code does not support

| Claim | Reality in `server/lib/cv/*.js` | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "The summary, the bullets **and the skills line** are rewritten for that job" | `tailor.js` rewrites summary, bullets, cover letter, screening — never the skills section | **Fix: implement, or drop "and the skills line"** |
| "cannot upgrade *supported* to *led* unless the source says so" | `refine.js` `WEAK_OPENERS` maps "was tasked with"→"Led", "helped with"→"Drove", "responsible for"→"Owned"; `assertGrounded` does not catch verb inflation | **Integrity defect: remove the inflections** |
| "All tailored content comes only from facts already in the CV" | `assertGrounded` skips sentence-initial proper nouns, lowercase skills ("microservices", "terraform"), and the number "one" | **Close the holes** |
| "Formatting is judged honestly" (10/10) | `extract.js` reads only `word/document.xml` — header/footer contact blocks are lost — and flattens table cells and tabs to spaces *before* `score.js` sees them | **Tables/two-column DOCX can score 10/10 while being unparseable** |
| Rubric weights 35/20/20/15/10 | enforced exactly, but keyword coverage is stemmed bigram overlap, while real ATS search is literal | Keep the weights, say "our rubric" (already does) |
| Ingestion list | `/how-it-works` and `/security` omit `.odt`, `.rtf`, `.html` and image OCR, which all ship | Copy drift |
| "No background polling" | `server/alerts-runner.js` runs a 30-minute in-process scan | Copy drift |
| Contact | `/about` links `mailto:hello@applywright.example` | Placeholder in production copy |

## 3. The grounded-rewrite gap

Across the nine commercial tools and the main open-source projects
([Resume-Matcher](https://github.com/srbhr/Resume-Matcher),
[RenderCV](https://github.com/rendercv/rendercv)), **none exposes a deterministic post-generation
verifier** that rejects fabricated numbers, employers, dates or qualifications. Commercial tools rely
on prompt instructions or actively reward ungrounded keyword insertion to move their match dial.

Methods that exist in the literature: **entity-set invariants** (`Entities(out) ⊆ Entities(source)` —
what our `assertGrounded` approximates), **atomic claim decomposition + NLI entailment**
([FActScore, EMNLP 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251);
[MiniCheck 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10774)) which catches verb and scope inflation that regexes
miss, and **slot-locked rewriting** that routes missing skills to a user prompt.

**Defensibility:** the code is a shallow moat (2–4 weeks to replicate), but the *position* is durable:
incumbents monetise synthetic score lifts ("41% → 88% in one click") that require injecting keywords the
CV never had. Strict refusal depresses their headline metric, so they are structurally slow to copy it —
provided we (a) close the regex holes above and (b) turn refusal into guided evidence prompts rather
than silence.

## 4. Trust and refusal UX

Our surfaces disagree: the home hero shows a crisp refusal (`finding · cadence`), `/editor` buries
refused hunks in a collapsed `<details>` without showing the rejected draft, and `/app` drops bullet
diffs and tailoring warnings entirely.

Patterns worth adopting: **refused-change cards** with the offending span struck through and the rule
named ("22% is absent from CV §Experience; kept original") — visible refusal is what proves the
guardrail; **evidence prompts** ("Refused to add 'SOC 2' — did you do this work? → add a grounded
bullet") in the GOV.UK error-summary spirit
([pattern](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/)); **provenance chips**
([`CV §2 · 18%`]) that highlight the supporting line on focus, in the manner of citation UIs
([Anthropic citations](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/citations)); and
**interactive abstention pills** for unstated salary/notice figures, with copy disabled until filled.

## 5. Pricing, churn and UK VAT

Active searches last 6–12 weeks and monthly SaaS in this category churns 25–40%, which is why Teal
flanks $29/mo with 7-day and 90-day passes and Rezi sells a $149 lifetime tier. Convert **after** an
ungated first score plus three full grounded packs; charge **per application pack**, not per edit
(charging per edit punishes the behaviour that improves outcomes — `arXiv:2509.25054` finds revision
time raises callbacks). Our ladder is £0 / £19 / £49, not the £0 / £9 / £19 / £39 the brief assumed.

**Compliance defect:** `/` and `/pricing` say "Prices exclude VAT". Automated CV scoring is an
electronically supplied digital service; UK B2C prices must be VAT-inclusive
([ASA CAP 3.18](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/03.html);
[VAT thresholds](https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration-thresholds);
[digital services](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-vat-rules-if-you-supply-digital-services-to-private-consumers)).
Either show VAT-inclusive prices (use a merchant of record) or take the pricing page down until ready.
Also: `Scale` (£49) sells `apiAccess` and `priorityRefresh`, which are declared but unenforced, and
`/pricing` marks "Export everything" as No for Free and Pro when `GET /api/export` is open to everyone.

## 6. Distribution

Reddit (`r/EngineeringResumes`, `r/UKJobs`, `r/cscareerquestionsuk`, `r/resumes`) removes
self-promotion but rewards **zero-signup diagnostic utilities** shared inside review threads — e.g. a
tool that shows how a two-column DOCX or a Word header block fails plain-text extraction.
[Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) suits MIT, zero-dependency,
deterministic products. LinkedIn recruiter carousels ("advert → standard AI fabrication struck through →
grounded refusal") earn dwell time. The highest-converting format is an **unauthenticated
paste-CV + paste-advert → score + one grounded redline + one refusal** widget on the home page, with
sign-up deferred until save or export.
